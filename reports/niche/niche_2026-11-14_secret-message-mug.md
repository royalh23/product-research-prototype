## Pick: Office Secret Santa box. Why
- **It's your angle, and the evidence fits it:** the funniest comments are workplace ones, suppliers already make "office/coworker" hidden-message mugs, and clean messages avoid Meta cutting reach of crude content.
- **The gift box fixes the margin:** a boxed, ready-to-gift mug can sell for $34.99 against their $27 bare mug, which is what makes 3x possible.
- **One-line hook:** "The Secret Santa gift that wins the office" shows in 3 seconds (tilt → reveal → laugh).

⚠️ **Seasonal flag:** the Secret Santa window closes in under 6 weeks. You'd need to post your first reel by about Nov 25. After Christmas, switch to niche 2 (farewell/new job), then niche 4 for Valentine's.

## 3 hook scripts
1. **Office reveal.** First 3 s: a coworker at a desk tilts the mug and the text appears (close-up). Text: "My Secret Santa gift to the coworker who replies-all". Middle: the box being opened, people around laughing, the 2nd message. CTA: "Comment **SANTA** · $34.99 gift-boxed" on screen.
2. **Unboxing (proven package-rip format).** First 3 s: a hand slices the gift-box ribbon. Text: "The $35 gift that won our office Secret Santa". Middle: tilt reveal, then show all 4 messages. CTA: "Comment **MUG** · $34.99 · arrives before Christmas" (only if CJ US shipping is confirmed).
3. **POV boss.** First 3 s: the boss holds the mug, tilts it, and frowns. Text: "POV: you gave this to your manager". Middle: the manager laughs, a thumbs up. CTA: "Comment **OFFICE** · 2 for $54.99".
