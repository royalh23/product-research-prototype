## Pick: Squad Pack. Why
- **The friend-tagging is the real driver.** The comments are laughing and tagging friends, and a 3-pack turns one viewer into 3 sales (higher order value).
- **Lasts longer:** ski season runs until March, while the gift angle dies around Dec 15. Run the gag-gift hook inside it until mid-December.
- **No IP and harder to compare:** mixed original funny faces in one bundle isn't a Minion knock-off and can't be found in 2 seconds.
