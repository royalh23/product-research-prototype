**Lessons learned during these hunts** (full list in `knowledge/lessons.md`):
- Shop badge = real order proof. Use the real Add to cart button; the cart API does not sync to Shop.
- Stores pre-select 2-packs and gifts, and KRW geolocation means prices are approximate.
- Sends > likes is the strongest early signal. Reels under 3 days old are still growing.
- Feed URL codes can lag by one reel, so verify a code on its own reel page before deep checks.
