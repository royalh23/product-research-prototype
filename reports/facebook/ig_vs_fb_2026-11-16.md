## What works better on Facebook
- **Gift / "send this to someone" hooks.** FB's 2nd, 3rd and 4th best reels are all "send this / don't let dog lovers see this" gift angles (2.9K, 2.2K, 2.0K), and the gift-box reel is the only one that beat Instagram (2.2K vs 1.5K). The FB audience seems to be buyers for someone else. This rests on **3 reels, under 5**, so it's a hint, not proof.
- **Security/airport drama works much worse on FB** than on IG (3–18x fewer views).
- Store-branded reels (ZARA, H&M, Target) did badly on FB: 9–647 views. They're also a brand risk.

## Where to post what
