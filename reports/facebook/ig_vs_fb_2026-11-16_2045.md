## What works better on Facebook
- **Gift-box reveal hooks.** Both gift-box reels beat Instagram, one by 12x (169 vs 2,000). Facebook's audience is older and buying gifts, which fits Q4.
- "Send this to a dog girl" (2,900) is also a gift and share hook.
- Skits with strangers, boyfriends or shops do badly on Facebook (6–265 views). The one exception is the security reel.
- Caveat: this is 2 gift reels, under 5, so treat it as a signal and not yet proof.

## Where to post what
- **Security/authority skits:** Instagram first (your viral format there).
- **Gift-box / "send this to…" reels:** Facebook first, then Instagram.
- **Store/couple skits:** Instagram only. They die on Facebook.
