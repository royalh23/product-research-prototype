## What worked: hooks to reuse in the next batch
1. **A "forbidden / secret" line aimed at a named group.** "HIDE this from the dog girlies" (61K) and "DON'T show this to dog girlies" (15.3K) are your top 2. The formula is *[hide / don't show] + [this] + [from a group that would want it]*. It makes viewers tag and send the reel to that group: the 61K reel has 41 shares, far more than any other reel.
2. **The "dog girlies" group beats "dog lovers".** Same clip and same structure: "dog girlies" got 61K and 15.3K, "dog lovers" got 6.7K. Keep the slang group name.
3. **Security / POV drama is still reliable.** It made 34K and 31K before and 6.8K now. Use it as the second format.
4. **The airport clip.** Every reel over 5K this week uses it. The bag looks like a real dog in a busy public place.

## What didn't work
- **Benefit or purchase lines** ("Never bought a backpack so FAST", 5.6K) and a **gift line** ("Your cousin needs this for Christmas", 7K) were mid-level. They're fine, but not outliers.
- About 4 reels were under 200 views. Posting 10 reels within 20 minutes (11-19) probably splits the reach. The new reels are only 1 day old, so check again on 11-22.
- Engagement rates are falling (likes −20%, comments −29%) as views grow. Very few people comment the keyword, so the CTA is not converting yet. Your bio still says "Shop below" but has **no link**.
