# Niche framework

Rauf's definition: **take a proven product and build a version of it for one specific buyer with one specific problem.** It doesn't compete with the original, buyers can't find it on Amazon or AliExpress in 2 seconds, and it can sell for 3x+.

Example he gave: a standard dog bed becomes a heated orthopedic bed with steps for older dogs with joint pain.

## The 4 levers (change at least one; usually audience plus one more)
1. **Audience:** a different person buys it. Example: a back cushion for long-haul drivers or new parents instead of office workers.
2. **Problem / use case:** the same product solves a different job. Example: the skull zip hoodie becomes a "lock-in" gym hoodie.
3. **Offer:** bundle, duo pack, gift box, guarantee, fast-shipping promise, price shown on screen.
4. **Version:** a small physical change the supplier already makes (size, material, lining, color, attachment).

## Where niche ideas come from (in order of strength)
1. **Complaints and questions in the competitor's comments.** Each one is an unmet need ("does it fit cats?", "too heavy", "ships to UK?").
2. **Who is tagging whom.** Moms tagging dads, girlfriends tagging boyfriends = a gift angle.
3. **Rising queries in Google Trends** (new names and use cases).
4. **Situations:** age (puppy/senior), place (car/travel/small apartment/outdoors), body (breed/size), identity (gym, gamer, nurse, biker), season (winter/Christmas).

## Rules
- The niche must be **sayable in one line**: "[product] for [specific person] who [problem]".
- The niche buyer must be **reachable with organic Reels**. There must be content and hashtags for that group.
- It must be possible to **show it in a 3-second hook.**
- **The supplier must already make it,** or it must need only light changes (packaging, bundle, insert card). Custom manufacturing is out unless Rauf says otherwise.
- **No IP.** An original design only.
- Prefer buyers with money (adults, parents, gift-givers) over buyers without it (young teens).

## Output format for /niche
| Niche | One-liner | Lever(s) | Evidence (comment, trend, tag) | Hook idea | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then: **Pick one** and say why in 3 bullets. Add 3 hook scripts (first 3 seconds + on-screen text + CTA keyword), supplier search terms, and the target price.

## Worked niches (reference)
- **Skull zip face hoodie** (proven by bck_2_ftr, Call of Duty IP): "Lock-In" gym hoodie (zip up = beast mode, villain-arc trend, January gym season). Backups: faceless-creator hoodie, airsoft operator hoodie. Notes: the viral driver was the package-destruction reveal, not the hoodie, and the comments kept asking for the price and shipping.
- **Dog collar camera** (pawsgotwings): "Grow your dog's page" for pet content creators. Backups: outdoor-cat tracker cam ("where does my cat go all day", must be very light), hiking-dog "GoPro".
