# Safety rules (Instagram and every site)

Instagram does not publish its limits. They change with account age and behaviour. These numbers are deliberately conservative.

## Always
- **Burner account only.** Never Rauf's business Instagram or Facebook. If the browser shows his business account, STOP and tell him.
- **Read only.** Never like, follow, comment, save, share, DM, post, or click "Shop now / Buy".
- Add to cart is allowed ONLY for the Shop check (quantity 1, then remove). Checkout, Buy now and any payment step are never allowed.
- **Never type a password** or fill in login, payment or verification forms. If a login screen appears, stop and ask Rauf to log in himself.
- **Never run browser work in parallel** (no subagents using the browser at the same time).
- Read numbers from page text or the accessibility tree first. Screenshots are slower and use more of Rauf's usage.

## Pace on Instagram
- Wait a random 2–6 seconds between actions. Scroll slowly.
- **One batch = at most 40 product reels or 70 reels viewed in total, whichever comes first. Then stop and wait for Rauf.**
- At most 15 profile pages per batch, and 40 reels per profile Reels tab.
- At most 3 batches a day, with at least 30–60 minutes between them.

## STOP right away and tell Rauf if you see
- A captcha, "suspicious activity", "try again later", "action blocked", "we restrict certain activity", or a forced login
- The page keeps failing to load (2 tries max, then stop)

## Other sites
- Meta Ad Library, Google Trends, Shopify stores and AliExpress: same pacing. Don't hammer search (at most about 20 searches per batch).
- If a site blocks you, record "blocked" in the data and move on. Never try to get around it.
