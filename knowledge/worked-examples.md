# Worked examples (calibration)

Real calls from 2026-11-14. Read them before scoring so your judgement matches Rauf's.

## 1. Skull zip hoodie, @bck_2_ftr: NICHE-ONLY (score 83, but hard kill: IP)
- Data: 60 reels; page median 35K; best reels 5.9M and 5.7M; 424 of 477 comments showed buying intent (230 were just the keyword "COD"); top reel had 27.6K shares on 83.6K likes; posts 2–4 reels a day; 4+ other accounts sell the same hoodie.
- Score: A = 12 + 8 + 5 (CROWDED) + 0 (no ads found) = 25 · B = 15 (momentum 3.1) + 10 (recent outliers) = 25 · C = 15 · D = 6 (0.33) · E = 12 (sizing −3) → **83**.
- Hard kill: Call of Duty / "Ghost" trademark on the product and packaging → **niche-only** (original design, new buyer).
- **Lesson (dates):** on Nov 14 the 8 newest reels showed 2.4K–81K views and looked like the trend was dying. Decoding the dates showed 5 of them were under 3 days old and still growing. The real momentum was 3.1x (rising). **Never judge a page before decoding dates.**
- **Lesson (format):** all 16 outliers opened on a hand cutting or smashing a sealed package. Reels that opened on a model got 2–40K views. The hook text was the same on hits and flops, so the first-frame action made the difference.
- **Lesson (comments):** 41 asked the price (never shown) and 41 asked about shipping to their country. Gaps like these are the niche opening.
